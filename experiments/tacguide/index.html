A robot policy that feels an object through tactile sensors can teach a policy that only sees it. This page tests whether the difference between the two can be amplified, the way classifier-free guidance amplifies the effect of a text prompt.

能通过触觉传感器感受物体的机器人策略，可以用来教一个只能看的策略。这一页检验两者之间的差异能否被放大，做法与 classifier-free guidance 放大文本提示的作用相同。

Setup设置 #

The baseline is OpenVTLA, the policy released with the OpenViTac benchmark: π0.5 with tactile tokens from AnyTouch 2 placed before its visual tokens, trained by flow matching with the released recipe. Next to it we train a vision-only π0.5 on the same demonstrations, and we combine the two velocity predictions as v=vvision+w (vtouch−vvision)v = v_{\text{vision}} + w\,(v_{\text{touch}} - v_{\text{vision}})v=vvision​+w(vtouch​−vvision​), where w=1w = 1w=1 gives back the policy with touch and w>1w > 1w>1 extrapolates beyond it. The combination either runs at test time or is distilled into a vision-only student, which then needs no tactile sensor. Every policy trains on the released demonstrations of Grasp Chip, a task in which the robot picks up a fragile potato chip and sets it on a tray, for 10k steps with 64 samples per step. The metric is closed-loop success in the OpenViTac simulator, judged by the benchmark's own task check. The code is a fork of OpenViTac, and the record links the commit each run started from.

Details

The policy with touch receives two camera images and, for each of the two fingertip sensors, a clip of four tactile frames. The vision-only policies receive the two camera images. As in the released configuration, no policy receives the robot's joint state.

A policy predicts 50 actions at a time. The simulator executes the first 20 and then asks for a new prediction.

The distilled student starts from the same weights and uses the same optimiser as the vision-only policy. In each training step the frozen policy with touch and the student see the same augmented images, and the student regresses the teacher's velocity.

A new scene is one that the simulator samples with a seed outside the range used for the demonstrations. A training scene uses a seed from the range that the demonstrations were collected with.

About a third of the simulator launches start in a broken state. An episode is kept only if, at its first step, both tactile images show all of their markers and the wrist image has the brightness of the training data. Otherwise the episode is run again.

基线是随 OpenViTac benchmark 发布的策略 OpenVTLA：在 π0.5 的视觉 token 之前加入 AnyTouch 2 提取的触觉 token，按官方配方用 flow matching 训练。我们在同一批演示上再训练一个纯视觉的 π0.5，并把两者预测的速度组合为 v=vvision+w (vtouch−vvision)v = v_{\text{vision}} + w\,(v_{\text{touch}} - v_{\text{vision}})v=vvision​+w(vtouch​−vvision​)，其中 w=1w = 1w=1 就是带触觉的策略，w>1w > 1w>1 则向外推。这个组合可以在测试时直接使用，也可以蒸馏进一个纯视觉学生，学生不再需要触觉传感器。所有策略都在官方发布的 Grasp Chip 演示上训练 1 万步（这个任务要求机器人夹起一片易碎的薯片并放到托盘上），每步 64 个样本。指标是 OpenViTac 仿真器中的闭环成功率，由 benchmark 自带的任务判定给出。代码是 OpenViTac 的一个 fork，实验记录里给出了每个 run 开始时所用的 commit。

详细说明

带触觉的策略输入两路相机图像，以及两个指尖传感器各四帧的触觉片段。纯视觉策略只输入两路相机图像。与官方配置一致，所有策略都不输入机器人的关节状态。

策略每次预测 50 个动作。仿真器执行前 20 个，然后请求新的预测。

蒸馏学生与纯视觉策略从相同的权重出发，使用相同的优化器。每个训练步里，冻结的带触觉策略和学生看到同样增强后的图像，学生回归教师的速度。

新场景指仿真器用演示采集范围之外的种子采样出的场景。训练场景使用的种子则落在演示采集的范围之内。

仿真器大约每三次启动就有一次处于异常状态。只有在第一步时两张触觉图像的标记点齐全、腕部图像亮度与训练数据一致的 episode 才会保留，否则重跑。

Experiments实验 #

Question 1问题 1 Answered已有结论How often do the policies complete Grasp Chip after 10k training steps?训练 1 万步后，各策略完成 Grasp Chip 的频率有多高？

Almost never: the policy with touch completes a single episode, and the vision-only policy and the distilled student complete none. The failures include scenes taken from the training demonstrations.

几乎从不：带触觉的策略只完成了一个 episode，纯视觉策略和蒸馏学生一个都没有完成。失败的 episode 里包括训练演示中出现过的场景。

Grasp Chip may be unable to show an effect of tactile guidance at all. An open issue on the benchmark reports that every learned policy its authors tried scores 0 of 50 on this task, and attributes this to two tolerances in the success check that the scripted expert never approaches.

The training loss gives no sign of the failure. Both policies trained on demonstrations converge smoothly.

Grasp Chip 可能根本测不出触觉引导的效果。benchmark 仓库里有一个未关闭的 issue，报告其作者试过的所有学习策略在这个任务上都是 50 次里 0 次成功，并认为原因是成功判定中的两个容差过严，而脚本专家从不会接近这两个容差。

训练损失看不出这种失败。两个用演示训练的策略都平稳收敛。

Question 2问题 2 Testing测试中Where do the failed grasps go wrong?失败的抓取错在哪里？

We measure how far from the centre of the chip the gripper first comes down. This distance does not separate success from failure: the one successful episode lands farther from the centre than the failed episodes of the same policy do on average.

我们测量夹爪第一次落下时离薯片中心有多远。这个距离并不能区分成败：唯一成功的那个 episode，落点比同一策略失败 episode 的平均落点离中心更远。

Details

On frames where the demonstrated arm is at rest, each predicted chunk still moves three joints by about 1 mrad, which shifts the gripper by about 0.4 mm. This offset does not depend on the position inside the chunk, on the sampled noise, on the numerical precision or on the number of denoising steps. The policy replans every 20 steps without seeing the joint state, so it cannot notice the drift.

The simulator executes the commands exactly: commanded and reached joint angles agree in free motion.

Without the renderer's upscaler the camera images are brighter and sharper than the training data, which moved the predicted grasp by up to 27 mm in an earlier round. Every episode on this page passed a brightness check on its first frame.

The demonstrations contain no second attempts.

详细说明

在演示中机械臂静止的帧上，每个预测的 action chunk 仍会让三个关节移动约 1 mrad，使夹爪偏移约 0.4 mm。这个偏移与 chunk 内的位置、采样的噪声、数值精度以及去噪步数都无关。策略每 20 步重新规划一次，又看不到关节状态，因此无法察觉这种漂移。

仿真器对指令的执行是精确的：自由运动时，指令关节角与实际到达的关节角一致。

不启用渲染器的超分辨率时，相机图像比训练数据更亮、更锐，在更早的一轮评测中使预测的抓取位置最多偏了 27 mm。本页的每个 episode 都通过了第一帧的亮度检查。

演示里没有第二次尝试。

Takeaways结论 #

Related work相关工作 #

  • OpenViTac, Wu et al. 2026. A benchmark of 11 visuo-tactile manipulation tasks in simulation with paired real-world tasks, released with the OpenVTLA policy. Our policy with touch is OpenVTLA trained with its released recipe, and all evaluations use its simulator.
  • π0.5, Physical Intelligence 2025. A vision-language-action model that generates action chunks by flow matching. It is the backbone of every policy on this page.
  • AnyTouch 2, Feng et al. 2026. A tactile encoder for optical sensors that represents short clips of tactile frames. OpenVTLA uses it to turn touch into tokens.
  • Classifier-free guidance, Ho and Salimans 2022. Extrapolates from an unconditional prediction towards a conditional one to strengthen the condition. We apply the same rule with touch as the condition.
  • OpenViTac，Wu 等，2026。 一个包含 11 个视触觉操作任务的仿真 benchmark，并配有对应的真机任务，同时发布了 OpenVTLA 策略。我们带触觉的策略就是按官方配方训练的 OpenVTLA，所有评测都使用它的仿真器。
  • π0.5，Physical Intelligence，2025。 用 flow matching 生成 action chunk 的 vision-language-action 模型。本页所有策略都以它为骨干。
  • AnyTouch 2，Feng 等，2026。 面向光学触觉传感器的编码器，对短的触觉帧片段做表征。OpenVTLA 用它把触觉变成 token。
  • Classifier-free guidance，Ho 和 Salimans，2022。 从无条件预测向有条件预测外推，以加强条件的作用。我们用同样的规则，把触觉当作条件。
