Video world models can hold a scene together when a camera turns away and retraces its own path. This page tests whether they still return to the same place when the camera keeps turning in one direction through a full circle.

当相机转向一侧再沿原路退回时，视频世界模型往往能维持场景一致。这一页检验当相机朝同一方向持续旋转一整圈时，它们是否仍能回到同一个地方。

Setup设置 #

Each model starts from a single image and rotates the camera in place along two paired trajectories of equal frame count and equal total rotation: a one-way turn past 360 degrees, and an out-and-back turn that goes halfway and reverses. We evaluate five open video world models, WorldCrafter-Fast, HY-WorldPlay, Matrix-Game 3.0, SANA-WM and Matrix-Game 2.0, alongside rendered real 360-degree panoramas as a positive control, with memoryless Matrix-Game 2.0 serving as the lower reference. Every model runs on 52 scenes made of 12 synthetic pilot images and 40 real photographs, except WorldCrafter-Fast, which currently covers 32 scenes. We measure loop closure with human ratings on whether the starting view returns and with the arc score, which checks whether the starting view enters smoothly across the approach before the best revisit frame rather than appearing by an abrupt switch. All 488 rollouts sit in the public loop360 dataset, and the metric code lives in the loop360 repository.

Details

Because models do not follow commanded angles exactly, every one-way trajectory commands more than 360 degrees, ranging from 445 to 549 degrees across models, and the revisit frame is located from the video itself.

The arc score aligns each sampled frame to frame 0 with RootSIFT homographies, divides frame 0 into an 8 by 14 grid, and counts matchable cells confirmed by inliers. It normalizes the arrival stretch before the best revisit frame against the departure stretch right after frame 0 over the same camera travel, measured in image widths from frame-to-frame motion. A companion point score evaluates only the single best revisit frame.

All automatic scores on this page are computed on 640-pixel preview videos sampled every two frames, at a departure threshold of 0.15 unless stated otherwise. Two SANA-WM previews were unreadable and are excluded.

The real panorama control uses four CC0 panoramas rendered with pure rotation over 601 frames.

每个模型从同一张起始图像出发，让相机在原地沿两条帧数与总转角相同的轨迹旋转：一条是超过 360 度的单向旋转，另一条是转过一半后沿原路返回的往返旋转。我们评测了五个开源视频世界模型，分别是 WorldCrafter-Fast、HY-WorldPlay、Matrix-Game 3.0、SANA-WM 和 Matrix-Game 2.0，并以真实 360 度全景图渲染的视频作为正对照，其中没有记忆机制的 Matrix-Game 2.0 作为下界参照。除 WorldCrafter-Fast 目前覆盖 32 个场景外，其余模型均在 52 个场景上运行，包括 12 张合成起始图和 40 张实拍照片。我们通过人工评分与弧分数衡量回环一致性：前者判断起点画面是否重新出现，后者进一步检查最佳回访帧之前的接近段是否平滑接回，而非突然切回记忆中的画面。全部 488 条视频已放在公开的 loop360 数据集，评测代码位于 loop360 仓库。

详细说明

由于模型不会严格按指令角度旋转，所有单向轨迹都会下发超过 360 度的总转角，各模型在 445 至 549 度之间，回访帧由视频本身定位而非按指令推算。

弧分数先用 RootSIFT 单应矩阵将各采样帧与第 0 帧对齐，再把第 0 帧划为 8 × 14 的网格，统计可匹配格中被内点确认的比例。随后按帧间运动累积的图像宽度距离，把最佳回访帧前的接近段与起点后的离开段对齐并归一化。作为对照的点分数只计算最佳回访那一帧。

本页所有自动指标均在 640 像素宽的预览视频上每 2 帧采样计算，除非另有说明，离开阈值取 0.15。两条 SANA-WM 预览视频损坏无法读取，已排除。

真实全景对照使用四张 CC0 全景图，以纯旋转方式渲染 601 帧。

Experiments实验 #

Question 1问题 1 Answered已有结论Do video world models return to the starting view after a one-way full turn?单向旋转一整圈后，视频世界模型能回到起点画面吗？

Some models do, yet every memory-equipped model succeeds far less often on a one-way turn than on an out-and-back turn of the same length. In an initial single-rater pass over 19 scenes each for HY-WorldPlay and Matrix-Game 3.0, 15 for SANA-WM, all 52 for Matrix-Game 2.0 and all 4 panoramas, both HY-WorldPlay and Matrix-Game 3.0 return in 100% of out-and-back videos, whereas one-way returns drop to 52.6% for HY-WorldPlay and 0% for Matrix-Game 3.0.

部分模型可以，但所有带记忆的模型在单向旋转上的成功率都远低于同等长度的往返旋转。在首批单人评分中，HY-WorldPlay 和 Matrix-Game 3.0 各评了 19 个场景，SANA-WM 评了 15 个，Matrix-Game 2.0 为全部 52 个，真实全景为全部 4 个：HY-WorldPlay 与 Matrix-Game 3.0 在往返轨迹上均 100% 回到起点，但在单向轨迹上分别降到 52.6% 和 0%。

Details

These ratings come from a single rater who was not blind to the model, covering question 1 only: whether the video clearly returned to the starting place. WorldCrafter-Fast was generated after this rating pass and has no human scores here.

详细说明

这一批评分来自单人非盲测，只包含问题 1，即视频是否明确回到了起始地点。WorldCrafter-Fast 在本轮评分之后才生成，因此暂未计入。

Question 2问题 2 Answered已有结论How do the five models score on the arc metric across both trajectories?五个模型在两种轨迹上的弧分数表现如何？

The arc score reproduces the gap between out-and-back and one-way turns, and places WorldCrafter-Fast above the other models on one-way closure.

弧分数复现了往返与单向之间的落差，并且在单向回环上将 WorldCrafter-Fast 排在所有模型之首。

Comparing the single-frame point score with the arc score on one-way videos exposes cases where a model recovers the starting view abruptly rather than rotating into it smoothly.

在单向视频上对比单帧点分数与弧分数，可以筛出那些并非平滑转回起点、而是突然切回记忆画面的样本。

Details

SIFT matching penalizes returns whose fine texture was redrawn even when the layout matches; HY-WorldPlay one-way rollouts are affected most. Switching to a DINO patch aligner raises HY-WorldPlay one-way arc from 0.30 to 0.60, while keeping the same model ordering.

The arc score assumes pure rotation and under-scores rollouts where the camera drifts forward while turning.

详细说明

SIFT 匹配对细节重画的回访偏严，即使布局一致也会压低分数，HY-WorldPlay 的单向视频受此影响最大。换用 DINO patch 对齐器后，HY-WorldPlay 的单向弧分数从 0.30 升至 0.60，模型排序保持不变。

弧分数假设纯旋转，因此相机在旋转时向前漂移的视频会被低估。

Question 3问题 3 Answered已有结论Does the arc score agree with human ratings without false closures?弧分数是否与人工评分一致，且不产生误匹配？

On question 1 alone, SIFT arc and point scores reach nearly the same AUC at a departure threshold of 0.15, 0.967 versus 0.964, while SIFT keeps false closures on unrelated scenes at 4.1%. Because the existing human ratings ask only whether the starting view returned, they cannot yet separate arc from point; testing what arc adds requires question 2 in the blind evaluation below.

仅看问题 1 时，SIFT 离开阈值 0.15 下弧分数与点分数的 AUC 几乎相同，分别为 0.967 与 0.964，同时 SIFT 在错配场景上的误回环率保持在 4.1%。由于现有人工评分只问起点是否重现，还无法区分弧分数相对点分数的增益，这一点留待下方的双问题盲测检验。

Pairing each video against another scene's first frame tests how often the aligner claims a match where none exists. DINO produces far more false matches than SIFT at low thresholds.

将每条视频与另一个场景的第 0 帧配对，可以检验对齐器在不存在回环时有多容易误报。在低阈值下，DINO 的误匹配率远高于 SIFT。

Details

Negative control pairs each rollout with the next scene in alphabetical order. A few consecutive real photographs were taken at the same location, which may account for part of the remaining false closures at 0.15.

详细说明

负对照将每条视频与按文件名排序的下一个场景配对。少量相邻编号的实拍照片取自同一地点，可能贡献了阈值 0.15 处的部分残留误报。

Takeaways结论 #

Related work相关工作 #

  • WorldCrafter, Yu et al. 2026. A video world model with camera-queryable implicit 3D-aware memory for consistent scene exploration. In our benchmark it achieves the highest one-way arc score among the tested models, while also showing the widest gap between point and arc scores.
  • HY-WorldPlay, Sun et al. 2025. A real-time interactive world model using reconstituted context memory and context forcing for long-term geometric consistency. It returns reliably on out-and-back turns and on roughly half of one-way turns.
  • Matrix-Game 3.0, Wang et al. 2026. A streaming interactive world model with camera-aware memory retrieval and injection. It closes every rated out-and-back loop yet fails on one-way loops, showing that retracing a path is a much weaker test of spatial memory than closing a circle.
  • SANA-WM, Zhu et al. 2026. A hybrid linear diffusion transformer for minute-scale world modeling with 6-DoF camera control. We also reuse 12 synthetic start images from its benchmark as our pilot scenes.
  • WorldCrafter，Yu 等，2026。 带有相机可查询隐式 3D 记忆的视频世界模型，面向一致的场景探索。在我们的测试中，它在已测模型里单向弧分数最高，同时点分数与弧分数的差距也最大。
  • HY-WorldPlay，Sun 等，2025。 采用重构上下文记忆与 context forcing 的实时交互世界模型，旨在维持长期几何一致。它在往返旋转上稳定回环，在单向旋转上约有一半成功。
  • Matrix-Game 3.0，Wang 等，2026。 具备相机感知记忆检索与注入机制的流式交互世界模型。它在所有已评分的往返旋转上都能回环，却在单向旋转上全部失败，说明原路返回对空间记忆的检验远弱于闭环旋转。
  • SANA-WM，Zhu 等，2026。 支持 6-DoF 相机控制的混合线性扩散 Transformer 世界模型。我们还复用了其 benchmark 中的 12 张合成起始图作为先导场景。
