Setup设置 #

The baseline is the ODE initialization of Self Forcing: before the main distillation, a four-step causal video student learns to regress the ODE trajectories of its teacher. We follow the CausVid protocol on the 16k trajectory pairs that the Self Forcing authors released. We change the regression target: Swift replaces the plain target yyy with y+α (y−Gweak)y + \alpha\,(y - G_{\text{weak}})y+α(y−Gweak​), where GweakG_{\text{weak}}Gweak​ is the output of a frozen early checkpoint of the same student and α=0.25\alpha = 0.25α=0.25. The two arms share their first 750 updates, and each then trains to 3000 updates. We compare the VBench scores of the two students, at the end of the ODE initialization and again after DMD training.

Details

The student starts from the Wan2.1 text-to-video 1.3B weights. It uses the timesteps 1000, 750, 500 and 250, which a shift of 5 warps to the noise levels 1000, 937.5, 833.3 and 625. Training uses AdamW with a learning rate of 2e-6 and 32 samples per update, on four GPUs. The weak model is the shared checkpoint at update 750.

Each block of three latent frames gets one noise level at random. A block may also be clean, at level 0. A clean block serves as context for the later blocks and is left out of the loss.

For each checkpoint we generate the VBench videos with one fixed seed and score them on all 16 dimensions.

A first pair of runs trained without clean context blocks, although the sampler conditions on clean context at inference. We discarded those runs, and the record counts them as other compute.

基线是 Self Forcing 的 ODE 初始化：在正式蒸馏之前，一个四步的因果视频学生模型先学习回归教师模型的 ODE 轨迹。我们沿用 CausVid 的流程，数据是 Self Forcing 作者发布的 16k 对轨迹。我们只改回归目标：Swift 把原始目标 yyy 换成 y+α (y−Gweak)y + \alpha\,(y - G_{\text{weak}})y+α(y−Gweak​)，其中 GweakG_{\text{weak}}Gweak​ 是同一个学生模型早期 checkpoint 的输出，参数冻结，α=0.25\alpha = 0.25α=0.25。两组共用前 750 次更新，之后各自训练到 3000 次更新。我们比较两个学生模型的 VBench 分数：一次在 ODE 初始化结束时，一次在 DMD 训练之后。

详细说明

学生模型从 Wan2.1 文生视频 1.3B 的权重出发。它使用 timestep 1000、750、500 和 250，经 shift 5 变换后对应噪声水平 1000、937.5、833.3 和 625。训练使用 AdamW，学习率 2e-6，每次更新 32 个样本，使用四块 GPU。弱模型是共用阶段第 750 次更新的 checkpoint。

每个由三帧 latent 组成的块随机取一个噪声水平。一个块也可以是干净的，即噪声水平为 0。干净的块作为后面各块的上下文，不计入损失。

对每个 checkpoint，我们用一个固定的随机种子生成 VBench 视频，并在全部 16 个维度上评分。

最早的一对 run 在训练中没有干净的上下文块，而采样器在推理时以干净的上下文为条件。我们弃用了这两个 run，实验记录把它们计入其他算力。

Related work相关工作 #

  • Self Forcing, Huang et al. 2025. Trains an autoregressive video diffusion student on its own rollouts, so that training matches inference. Its student starts from the ODE initialization that we study here.
  • CausVid, Yin et al. 2025. Distills a bidirectional video diffusion teacher into a few-step causal student, with an ODE initialization before the main distillation. We keep that protocol and change only the target.
  • Swift. The target rule that we test. We apply it to the ODE initialization, with an early checkpoint of the student as the weak model.
  • Self Forcing，Huang 等，2025。 让自回归视频扩散学生模型在自己的 rollout 上训练，使训练与推理一致。它的学生模型从我们这里研究的 ODE 初始化出发。
  • CausVid，Yin 等，2025。 把双向视频扩散教师模型蒸馏成少步数的因果学生模型，并在正式蒸馏之前先做 ODE 初始化。我们沿用这一流程，只改回归目标。
  • Swift。 我们检验的目标规则。我们把它用在 ODE 初始化上，以学生模型的早期 checkpoint 作为弱模型。
