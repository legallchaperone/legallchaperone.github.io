PF-ODE #

Forward SDE dxt=ft(xt) dt+gt dWtdx_t = f_t(x_t)\,dt + g_t\,dW_tdxt​=ft​(xt​)dt+gt​dWt​, marginals ptp_tpt​.

dxtdt=ft(xt)−12gt2∇xlog⁡pt(xt)=:v⋆(xt,t)\dfrac{dx_t}{dt} = f_t(x_t) - \tfrac12 g_t^2\nabla_x\log p_t(x_t) =: v^\star(x_t, t)dtdxt​​=ft​(xt​)−21​gt2​∇x​logpt​(xt​)=:v⋆(xt​,t) has the same marginals ptp_tpt​ as the SDE.

Derivation:

  1. Fokker–Planck: ∂tpt=−∇⋅(ftpt)+12gt2Δpt\partial_t p_t = -\nabla\cdot(f_t p_t) + \frac12 g_t^2\Delta p_t∂t​pt​=−∇⋅(ft​pt​)+21​gt2​Δpt​.
  2. Δp=∇⋅(∇p)=∇⋅(p∇log⁡p)\Delta p = \nabla\cdot(\nabla p) = \nabla\cdot(p\nabla\log p)Δp=∇⋅(∇p)=∇⋅(p∇logp) ⇒ ∂tpt=−∇⋅[(ft−12gt2∇log⁡pt) pt]\partial_t p_t = -\nabla\cdot\big[(f_t - \frac12 g_t^2\nabla\log p_t)\,p_t\big]∂t​pt​=−∇⋅[(ft​−21​gt2​∇logpt​)pt​].
  3. This is the continuity equation of the ODE with velocity v⋆v^\starv⋆.
  • Same marginals, different trajectories (SDE random, ODE deterministic).
  • Check (1D, f=0f = 0f=0, g=1g = 1g=1, p0=N(0,σ02)p_0 = \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_0^2)p0​=N(0,σ02​)): v⋆=x2(σ02+t)v^\star = \frac{x}{2(\sigma_0^2+t)}v⋆=2(σ02​+t)x​ ⇒ x(t)=x0(σ02+t)/σ02x(t) = x_0\sqrt{(\sigma_0^2+t)/\sigma_0^2}x(t)=x0​(σ02​+t)/σ02​​ ⇒ variance σ02+t\sigma_0^2 + tσ02​+t ✓.

Distribution level: VSD #

Objective (match noisy marginals at all times):

LVSD(θ)=Et[ω(t) DKL(ptθ ∥ pt)] \mathcal{L}_{\text{VSD}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_t\Big[\omega(t)\,D_{\text{KL}}\big(p^\theta_t\,\|\,p_t\big)\Big] LVSD​(θ)=Et​[ω(t)DKL​(ptθ​∥pt​)]
  • ptθp^\theta_tptθ​: student samples noised to ttt. ptp_tpt​: data (teacher) noised to ttt.
  • Noise ⇒ full support ⇒ KL well defined (the "punchline" of part 2).

Gradient of a general f-divergence (x=Gθ(z)x = G_\theta(z)x=Gθ​(z), r=pθ/pr = p_\theta/pr=pθ​/p, sθ=∇log⁡pθs_\theta = \nabla\log p_\thetasθ​=∇logpθ​, s=∇log⁡ps = \nabla\log ps=∇logp):

Why KL is special:

  • Only KL has w≡1w \equiv 1w≡1 ⇒ pure score difference; both scores come from diffusion models.
  • rf′′=cr f'' = crf′′=c ⇒ f=c rlog⁡rf = c\,r\log rf=crlogr + linear ⇒ KL is the unique such f-divergence.
  • Others need r(x)r(x)r(x) itself; scores give only ∇log⁡r\nabla\log r∇logr (no additive constant) ⇒ need a ratio estimator (discriminator).
  • Meaning of rrr: r>1r > 1r>1 student over-produces there; r<1r < 1r<1 under-covers. w(r)w(r)w(r) = how loudly each sample speaks.
  • KL(pθ∥p)\mathrm{KL}(p_\theta\|p)KL(pθ​∥p) (student first) is mode-seeking; f-distill calls it "reverse KL" (its convention puts the teacher first).

SDS vs. VSD: SDS (DreamFusion) replaces the student score with the injected noise ⇒ oversaturated, low diversity. VSD (ProlificDreamer, originally text-to-3D) learns the student score instead.

DMD (CVPR 2024): L=LDM+λ Lreg\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{DM}} + \lambda\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{reg}}L=LDM​+λLreg​.

  • Lreg=E(z,y)LPIPS(Gθ(z),y)\mathcal{L}_{\text{reg}} = \mathbb{E}_{(z,y)}\mathrm{LPIPS}(G_\theta(z), y)Lreg​=E(z,y)​LPIPS(Gθ​(z),y), y=Ψ1→0(z)y = \Psi_{1\to0}(z)y=Ψ1→0​(z) precomputed by the teacher's deterministic ODE sampler.
  • = KD on a fixed subset ⇒ fixes the coupling; counters mode collapse of the mode-seeking KL.
  • Cost: millions of teacher samples; student capped by teacher.

DMD2 (NeurIPS 2024):

  • Remove regression loss.
  • Instability came from an inaccurate fake score ⇒ two time-scale update rule: update sζs_\zetasζ​ more often than GθG_\thetaGθ​.
  • GAN loss: discriminator head on the fake score network, real vs. fake noised samples, trained on real data ⇒ can surpass teacher.
  • Multi-step: backward simulation (train on the student's own intermediate inputs).

f-distill (2025): DMD2 + weighting.

  • Reuse the GAN discriminator to estimate rrr: r^=(1−D)/D\hat r = (1-D)/Dr^=(1−D)/D (student/teacher; paper defines rrr inversely).
  • g=sg(w(r^))⋅(sζ−steacher)g = \mathrm{sg}\big(w(\hat r)\big)\cdot(s_\zeta - s_{\text{teacher}})g=sg(w(r^))⋅(sζ​−steacher​).
  • JS and forward KL beat reverse-KL VSD on ImageNet-64 and MS-COCO.
  • Cost: weights depend on discriminator accuracy; extreme DDD ⇒ extreme weights.

Training step (f-distill; set w=1w = 1w=1 for DMD2):

x   = G_θ(z);  t ~ p(t);  ε ~ N(0, I);  x_t = α_t·x + β_t·ε
s_real = teacher(x_t, t)        # frozen
s_fake = fake_score(x_t, t)     # trained by DSM on student samples
D      = disc(x_t, t)           # P(real); trained by BCE on real vs. student
r̂ = (1 − D)/D;  w = sg(r̂·f''(r̂))
g = w·(s_fake − s_real)
loss_G = ½‖x − sg(x − g)‖²  (+ GAN generator loss)
